Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our services

Do you handle COD (Cash on Delivery) orders?

Yes, absolutely. We provide 100% secure COD handling with instant verification, transparent reconciliation reports, and daily payment settlements. Every transaction is tracked and reported.

Can you pick up from my store or warehouse?

Yes, we offer scheduled pickups from your location. Call us to schedule a pickup slot, and our team will collect packages at your convenience within 2 hours.

What areas do you cover?

We primarily serve Shahabad taluka with same-day and next-day delivery. We also cover surrounding villages within 15km radius. Contact us to confirm delivery to your specific location.

How do I track my shipments?

Every shipment gets a unique tracking ID. You'll receive real-time SMS and WhatsApp updates at each milestone — pickup, in-transit, and delivery confirmation with photo proof.

What happens if a package is damaged?

We handle all packages with extreme care and insure high-value items. If damage occurs, we document it immediately with photos and file a claim within 24 hours.

Do you offer return/reverse pickup services?

Yes, we specialize in reverse pickups. We collect returns from customers, scan them, verify conditions, and handle reconciliation automatically. Daily reports included.
